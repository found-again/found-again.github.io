About this project

These picture books tell how our ancestors joined The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and the stories of the missionaries who left their homes to find them. They were made for our family: children, grandchildren, and cousins near and far.

Each book is built from the records: the 1898 Bitterroot register, family histories and journals, mission reports, obituaries, and the memories kept on FamilySearch. The appendix at the back of every book lists the sources page by page and notes where the records disagree.
